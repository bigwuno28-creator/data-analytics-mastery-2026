# Day 01 — Tableau: Superstore Data Exploration

## Dataset

Sample Superstore

## Objective

The objective of this exercise was to begin developing
fundamental Tableau data visualization skills using a
realistic retail dataset.

## Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Category

Analyzed total sales across product categories.

### 2. Profit by Category

Compared profitability across categories.

### 3. Sales Over Time

Analyzed sales trends across the order timeline.

### 4. Sales by State

Used geographic visualization to examine sales distribution.

### 5. Sales vs Profit

Explored the relationship between sales and profitability.

## Key Concepts Practiced

- Dimensions
- Measures
- Rows and Columns
- Bar charts
- Line charts
- Geographic maps
- Scatter plots
- Sorting
- Filters
- Labels
